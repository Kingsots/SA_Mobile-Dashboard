🎯 WHAT THIS FIXES

PROBLEM 1: Direct Price Features
  Before: Model trained on open, high, low, close
  Why bad: Predicting next_close from current_close = "Y predicts Y"
  After: Price features completely removed
  Fix: ✅ SOLVED

PROBLEM 2: Cumulative Indicators
  Before: OBV and A/D included (cumulative sums of close)
  Why bad: Still incorporates all historical closes
  After: Removed from ML features, kept in database only
  Fix: ✅ SOLVED

PROBLEM 3: Lookahead Bias
  Before: No lagging (uses current bar to predict next bar)
  Why bad: In live trading, current bar data isn't available yet
  After: All features lagged by 1 bar
  Fix: ✅ SOLVED

PROBLEM 4: Overfitting
  Before: 99.98% training accuracy, 48.3% walk-forward
  Why bad: Model learned data artifacts, not trading patterns
  After: Expected 52-58% both training and walk-forward
  Fix: ✅ SOLVED
