⚡ KEY INSIGHTS

Why Lag(1)?
  • In live trading, at close of 13:00, you only know data through 12:59
  • Using 13:00 data to predict 13:01 = impossible, lookahead bias
  • lag(1) forces model to use only historical data available at prediction time

Why Remove Prices?
  • open/high/low/close directly correlate with next close
  • Using close to predict next close = using X to predict Y when Y is X+noise
  • Model learns: "next_close ≈ current_close" (worthless for trading)

Why OBV/A/D Removed (but kept in database)?
  • These are cumulative sums of volume × price data
  • They incorporate the full historical close prices
  • lag(1) doesn't help: the cumulative value still includes current bar
  • Safe for ATR calculations, not safe for ML prediction

Why Keep VWAP_SLOPE?
  • VWAP = absolute level (incorporates close)
  • VWAP_SLOPE = rate of change (relative metric)
  • Rate of change is independent of absolute price level
  • Safer for prediction
